The Story

Music with something real behind it.

Brian Ansel Major is an independent artist building his catalog one song at a time. His work reflects personal experience, emotion and the reality of life outside the spotlight.

His music is rooted in authenticity — songs written to connect with people who have lived through love, loss, setbacks, change and the determination to keep moving forward.

Every song is another piece of the story.

The goal is simple: make music that feels honest, connects with people and gives listeners something they can carry with them.

About the Artist

Authenticity first.

Rather than chasing a manufactured image, Brian's music is focused on genuine emotion, personal experience and the stories that come from real life.

Real Life

Music shaped by experiences, emotions, challenges and the moments that leave a lasting impression.

Keep Moving

Songs about resilience, change and continuing forward when life doesn't always go according to plan.
