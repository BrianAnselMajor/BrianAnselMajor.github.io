The Story

Music with something real behind it.

Brian Ansel Major is an independent artist building his catalog one song at a time. His work reflects personal experience, emotion and the reality of life outside the spotlight.

Authenticity first.

Rather than chasing a manufactured image, Brian's music is rooted in authenticity — songs written to connect with people who have lived through love, loss, setbacks, change and the determination to keep moving forward.

Every song is another piece of the story.

Real life creates the stories. Music gives those stories a voice.
