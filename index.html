The Story

Music rooted
in real life.

“Songs written to connect with people who have lived through love, loss, setbacks, change and the determination to keep moving forward.”


Brian Ansel Major is an independent artist building his catalog one song at a time. His music reflects personal experience, emotion and the reality of life outside the spotlight.


Rather than chasing a manufactured image, the focus is on authenticity — creating songs that people can hear, feel and connect with.
