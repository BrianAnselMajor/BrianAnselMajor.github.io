The Story

Behind
the music.

Not every story is easy to talk about. Some are easier to put into a song.

Brian Ansel Major is an independent recording artist and songwriter from Des Arc, Arkansas. After going through major setbacks and rebuilding his life, he turned to music to express what he couldn't always say in conversation.

His songs move between country-influenced storytelling, Southern rap, hip-hop, and emotionally driven writing. Relationships, loss, difficult decisions, memories, and hard-earned lessons shape the music.

Brian writes his lyrics and records his vocals, building his catalog independently through Major Scars Records. He makes music to connect with listeners who recognize something of their own lives in a song—not to fit a manufactured image.

Short artist biography: Brian Ansel Major is an independent recording artist and songwriter from Des Arc, Arkansas, and the founder of Major Scars Records. Drawing on lived experience, his original music ranges from heartfelt country-influenced songs to Southern rap and hip-hop. His songwriting explores relationships, loss, personal setbacks, and moving forward.

Music is both his creative outlet and a way to connect with people who have their own stories to tell. Brian works independently, writing his lyrics and recording his vocals.
