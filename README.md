Official website for Brian Ansel Major

Independent Recording Artist | Songwriter | Musician

Founder of Major Scars Records

Brian Ansel Major is an independent recording artist and songwriter from Des Arc, Arkansas, whose music is rooted in personal experience, hard lessons, and the realities of rebuilding a life after losing nearly everything.

For Major, music began as something more personal than a career ambition. After going through a difficult period in his life, he found that writing and recording songs gave him a way to express things he struggled to put into ordinary conversation.

Rather than chasing fame or fortune, Major approaches music as a form of personal freedom. His songwriting draws heavily from his own experiences, exploring relationships, mistakes, memories, heartbreak, personal struggles, and the determination to keep moving forward.

Working independently, Major writes his own lyrics, develops his music, and records his own vocals. His catalog moves between heartfelt country-influenced songs, Southern storytelling, and more aggressive rap and hip-hop, reflecting the different sides of his personality and experiences.

Major also founded Major Scars Records, an independent record label built around his belief that the experiences that leave their mark on a person can also become the foundation for something meaningful.

His growing catalog includes songs such as Everybody Changes, How Many Times, Every Damn Bit of My Love, and I'll Wait for You.

At the center of his work is a straightforward philosophy: his music isn't meant to appeal to everybody. It's meant to be an honest reflection of his life, written and performed in his own words.
